About Us

Built to Support Global Businesses with Filipino Talent

Cancare Connect is a Philippines-based outsourcing company built to help global businesses scale with trained, reliable, and managed Filipino professionals.

Our Story

Cancare Connect was created to make outsourcing more reliable, personal, and performance-driven. Based in the Philippines, we connect global businesses with skilled professionals who can support customer service, appointment setting, lead follow-up, virtual assistance, and back-office operations.

Our approach is built around clear communication, daily management, and service quality, giving clients the support they need without the complexity of hiring and managing in-house.

Our Process

Built for Reliable Client Support

Cancare Connect follows a structured onboarding and production process to help every support team understand the client's business, tools, workflows, and service standards before moving into full operations.

01

Client-Led Training

Your team provides the product, process, and service training needed for the assigned role.

02

Operations Review

Our operations team reviews your materials and prepares the team for training, nesting, and production.

03

Nesting Phase

Agents go live in a monitored environment while performance, communication, and process alignment are reviewed.

04

Production Support

Once ready, the team is endorsed to production with supervision, quality monitoring, and reporting.
